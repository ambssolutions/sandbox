Project Management

Design, consent and construction coordinated with one accountable team. One point of contact keeps every consultant, council step and deadline moving together.

We coordinate the full subdivision design, including resource consent (RC), building consent (BC) and engineering plan approval (EPA), so the design, drawings and council approvals line up.

Coordinated together

  • Resource consent (RC)
  • Building consent (BC)
  • Engineering plan approval (EPA)

One team

Design, drawings and council approvals line up because the same team prepares them.

We manage the construction stage on your behalf, keeping the programme, contractors, quality, inspections and variations under control.

We keep control of

  • The programme
  • Contractors
  • Quality
  • Inspections and variations

The result

The build stays on track and you have one point of contact on site.

We manage communication and consultation with neighbours, council and other affected parties, so concerns are heard early and the project keeps moving.

Who we deal with

  • Neighbours
  • Council
  • Other affected parties

Why it helps

Concerns are heard early and addressed, so the project keeps moving.

We administer your construction contracts, including payments, variations, programme and compliance with the contract terms.

We administer

  • Payments
  • Variations
  • Programme
  • Compliance with contract terms

The result

Clear records and fewer disputes during construction.

We prepare the tender documents and manage the tender process, from inviting contractors through to comparing prices and recommending a contractor.

The process

  • Prepare tender documents
  • Invite contractors
  • Compare prices
  • Recommend a contractor

The result

A fair, well-documented tender and a confident choice of contractor.

We act as your engineer's representative on site, reviewing the works against the approved designs and council requirements.

On site we

  • Review works against the approved designs
  • Check council requirements are met
  • Report back to you

Why it matters

Independent oversight that the work is built as designed.

One team

One point of contact for every consultant, every council step and every deadline.
