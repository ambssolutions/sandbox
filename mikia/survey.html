Survey

Topographic, title and boundary survey work. Accurate site information is the base for good design, so we start from the ground and work up.

A topographic survey records the levels, boundaries, buildings, services and features on your site. It gives you an accurate base for design, planning and engineering.

What is recorded

  • Levels and contours
  • Boundaries
  • Buildings
  • Services and site features

What you get

An accurate base plan that designers, planners and engineers can all work from.

We prepare and lodge the survey plan for approval under section 223 of the Resource Management Act, a key step on the way to new titles for a subdivision.

What we do

  • Prepare the survey plan
  • Lodge it for approval under section 223
  • Keep the process moving with council

Where it fits

Section 223 is a key step on the way to new titles for a subdivision.

Help where a title has an error or does not match what is on the ground, such as an incorrect area, boundary or easement, and the survey steps needed to put it right.

Typical problems

  • An incorrect area
  • A boundary that does not match the ground
  • An easement error

How we help

We work out what is wrong and the survey steps needed to put it right.

Survey and plan preparation for adjusting the boundary between adjoining titles, taken through approval and the issue of new titles.

What is included

  • Survey and plan preparation
  • Approval through council
  • Issue of new titles

Working with planning

Planning advice and survey work are handled together, so the adjustment is not held up between consultants.
