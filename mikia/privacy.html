Privacy

How this website handles your information.

What we collect

If you send an enquiry through this website, we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number if you give one, project type, site address if you give one, and your message.

How we use it

We use these details only to reply to your enquiry and to discuss your project. We do not sell your information.

Third parties

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads. If you book a meeting, you leave this website for Google Calendar, which has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you. Email info@mikia.co.nz and we will respond.
