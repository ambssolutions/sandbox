COA (Certificate of Acceptance)

A Certificate of Acceptance (COA) is issued by council for building work that was done without a required building consent. We prepare the information council needs and take the application through to approval.

If building work was done without a required consent, we guide you through getting it formally recognised.

  • Review of the work and what council will need
  • Information council needs, prepared for you
  • Application lodged and managed with council
  • Questions from council answered on your behalf
  • Application taken through to approval
