All questions
What does MiKia do?

MiKia Consulting Group is a fully integrated land development consultancy in Auckland. We combine project management, planning, land surveying, engineering, environmental advice and landscape architecture in one team.

Who do you work with?

Landowners and developers with residential or commercial sites, from a single subdivision or infill project to larger neighbourhoods and mixed-use developments.

How do I get started?

Book a meeting with Neelam Gandhi or send us an enquiry with your site address and what you would like to do. We will come back to you quickly to talk through your goals.

What happens after I enquire?

We work in four stages: understand the site, design and consent, deliver on site, then complete and hand over. You keep one point of contact the whole way.
