Stormwater Design

Effective stormwater management is an important part of any successful development.

Effective stormwater management is an important part of any successful development. As sites become more developed and impervious areas increase, the volume and rate of stormwater runoff can also increase, placing additional pressure on existing drainage networks and receiving environments.

At MIKIA Design and Construction Group, we provide practical stormwater design solutions for residential developments, subdivisions and commercial projects. We assess the site's existing conditions, proposed development, catchment characteristics and discharge requirements to develop stormwater systems that manage runoff effectively and meet relevant council requirements.

01

Stormwater Conveyance Design

We design stormwater conveyance systems to safely collect and direct runoff from roofs, driveways, roads and other impervious areas. Solutions may include piped networks, channels, swales, culverts and other drainage infrastructure, depending on the site and project requirements.

02

Stormwater Quantity Management

Development can change both the volume and rate of stormwater runoff. We design retention and detention solutions to help manage these changes and control stormwater discharge. Depending on the site, solutions may include detention tanks, retention systems and other stormwater management devices.

03

Stormwater Quality Treatment

Stormwater can carry sediment, metals, hydrocarbons and other contaminants from developed surfaces into the receiving environment. We design treatment solutions to reduce these contaminants before stormwater is discharged, with the treatment approach selected according to the site, catchment and receiving environment.

04

Rain Garden Design

Rain gardens are a practical water-sensitive design solution that can help treat and manage stormwater close to where runoff is generated. They use specially selected soils and vegetation to filter and treat stormwater while also providing an attractive landscape feature. We can incorporate rain gardens and other bioretention systems into residential and development projects where they provide an appropriate stormwater management solution. Auckland Council's stormwater guidance identifies rain gardens and bioretention devices as options for stormwater treatment and hydrological management.

05

Wetland Design

Constructed wetlands can provide both stormwater treatment and flow management while creating additional ecological and landscape benefits. They can be particularly useful for larger developments where sufficient land is available for a treatment and detention system. We consider factors such as catchment area, inflows, water quality requirements, storage, vegetation, maintenance and discharge conditions when developing wetland-based stormwater solutions. Auckland Council recognises constructed wetlands as a stormwater treatment option within a water-sensitive design approach.

06

Integrated Stormwater Solutions

We look at stormwater as part of the overall site design rather than as a standalone drainage system. Our solutions can integrate pipes, overland flow paths, tanks, rain gardens, wetlands, swales and other devices to manage stormwater quantity and quality while working with the site's natural characteristics.

07

Practical Stormwater Design from Assessment to Delivery

From initial assessment and calculations through to detailed design and documentation, we provide coordinated stormwater solutions that consider the development, the existing network and the receiving environment.
