Residential

From bare land to streets people want to live on.

We take residential land from first feasibility through to new titles: understanding what the site can become, getting the consents, and managing the work so homes can follow.

  • Feasibility and yield advice before you commit
  • Subdivision and resource consent applications
  • Engineering, roading and services design
  • Survey for title, so new lots are ready to sell or build on
