Our services

Project management, planning, land surveying, engineering, environmental and landscape architecture, all under one roof so nothing falls between consultants.

One person accountable for your programme, your budget and every consultant on the job.

  • Programme and milestones, from feasibility through to title
  • Budget tracking and reporting so there are no surprises
  • Coordination of every consultant and every council step
  • Management of contractors, inspections and variations on site
  • Final sign-offs and handover

Where it fits. Runs through all four stages. You deal with one point of contact for every consultant, council step and deadline.

Resource management advice and consent applications for subdivision and development.

  • Feasibility advice on what your land can become
  • Resource consent applications prepared and lodged with council
  • Subdivision and development planning
  • Council-ready applications that fit with the engineering and landscape design

Where it fits. Starts at Stage 01 when we test feasibility, and leads the consent work in Stage 02.

Topographic, boundary and set-out surveys through to land transfer and new titles.

  • Topographic surveys to understand the site
  • Boundary surveys
  • Set-out surveys for construction
  • Survey for title, land transfer and new titles

Where it fits. Begins with the site survey in Stage 01 and finishes with the survey for title in Stage 04.

Civil design for roads, earthworks and three-waters infrastructure, plus building consent support.

  • Civil design for roads
  • Earthworks design
  • Three-waters infrastructure design
  • Designs, drawings and approvals
  • Building consent support

Where it fits. Prepared with planning and landscape design in Stage 02, then supported through delivery in Stage 03.

Environmental assessments and management that keep your development on the right side of the rules.

  • Environmental assessments for consent applications
  • Environmental management during development
  • Advice on meeting the conditions that apply to your site

Where it fits. Informs feasibility in Stage 01, supports consents in Stage 02 and carries through delivery.

Streets, reserves and outdoor spaces designed so new communities feel like home.

  • Street design
  • Reserves and public open space
  • Outdoor spaces for new neighbourhoods
  • Landscape design prepared together with planning and engineering

Where it fits. Designed alongside planning and engineering in Stage 02 and delivered on site in Stage 03.
