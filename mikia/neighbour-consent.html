Neighbour’s Consent

Council may need written approval from neighbours and other affected parties. We prepare the information, approach them on your behalf and collect the signed approvals.

We handle the conversations clearly and courteously, so your application is not held up.

  • Affected parties identified
  • Information prepared for neighbours
  • Neighbours approached on your behalf
  • Signed approvals collected
  • Approvals supplied with your application
