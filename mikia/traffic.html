Traffic

Traffic reports and plan change assessments. We assess how a development affects the roads around it and prepare what council needs to see.

A traffic report assesses the effects of your development on the road network, including expected traffic, access, parking and safety. It supports your consent application.

The report considers

  • Expected traffic
  • Access
  • Parking
  • Safety

How it is used

It supports your consent application and answers the questions council usually raises about the road network.

Advice on how Auckland Council's Plan Change 79 (transport provisions) affects your project, including parking and transport rules, and what it means for your consent.

What we cover

  • Parking rules
  • Transport provisions
  • What they mean for your consent

Why it matters

Plan Change 79 can change what is required for parking and transport. We explain how it affects your project.
