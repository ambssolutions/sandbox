So, did you choose real estate?

I grew up in a property family. Forty years of kitchen-table deals, sold signs and Sunday open homes. I didn't choose this job. I was raised in it.

How did you end up in South Auckland?

I moved to New Zealand more than 15 years ago and made South Auckland home in 2019. Now I sell it, film it, and run the odd community fundraiser on weekends.

How do you say hello?

Depends who's at the door. Kia ora, Sat Sri Akal, Namaste. Then I ask what you're really hoping for, and I listen.

And the big goal?

Big. And I'm saying it out loud, so you can hold me to it.

Jot. · Issue 01Fine printp.20

Fine print

The fine print, in type you can read.

Is the appraisal actually free?

Yes. Free, in writing and backed by comparable local sales. No obligation. If there aren't good comparables, I'll explain that in writing too. No catch.

Do I have to dance on camera?

Only if you want to. Your home is the star. You approve everything before it posts, and nothing is advertised without your OK.

How often will I hear from you?

After every open home, and in writing every week. Faster if you text. I aim to reply within one business day.

Where can I get advice that isn't yours?

settled.govt.nz has free, independent guides to buying and selling.

What if I'm not happy?

Tell me first and I'll do my best to put it right. You can also use Barfoot & Thompson's complaints process, or contact the Real Estate Authority (REA).
