## Audience
Locals in Liverpool, Warwick Farm and nearby suburbs, mostly on phones, who need a rego check or a repair and want to call.

## What competitors do (researched 15 November 2026)
- Local sites (JD Automotive Moorebank, Preston Mechanical, Motorserve Liverpool, Lube Mobile, Enzo's) all have click-to-call, a book button, a services list and a short pink vs blue slip explainer.
- Only JD shows a pink slip price. Preston and Lube Mobile state wrong or contradictory validity periods. Several have template leftovers, typos, doorway suburb pages and reviews from other branches.
- MB Mechanical Repairs, the nearest direct rival in the same street, has no website of its own.
- The best sites elsewhere: Kwik Fit (lookup as the first step, reminders), Midas and Good News Garage (a plainly stated guarantee), Sage Auto (services described by symptom).

## How this site answers that
- Accurate, sourced NSW rules and the regulated fees, with links to nsw.gov.au.
- A two-question "Pink slip, blue slip or neither?" helper.
- Services described by what the driver notices.
- Live open or closed status, one phone number per action, no stock photos, no doorway pages.

## Design
"The inspection bay." Night charcoal with paper-white sections, the card's orange and red, square corners and hairlines. Archivo (condensed) for headings, IBM Plex Sans for text, both self-hosted.
Signature motion: the red car draws itself in the hero and an inspection light sweeps across it, lighting up the pink slip check points. Then a story-bar services showcase with seven animated scenes, sticky stacked job cards, the slip helper, slow folds in the FAQ and the live week strip.
