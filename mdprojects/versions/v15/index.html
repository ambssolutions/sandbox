Our process

Six steps. One team.

You always know what's happening next, what it costs and when it'll be done.

  1. 1

    Feasibility study

    Zoning, services, geotechnical conditions and council rules, checked before you commit.

  2. 2

    Resource & building consents

    Applications prepared, lodged and managed with Auckland Council on your behalf.

  3. 3

    Engineering & infrastructure

    Roads, stormwater, power, water and telecommunications planned with our consultants.

  4. 4

    Construction

    Our network of 100+ trusted contractors builds to plan, managed day to day.

  5. 5

    Compliance & certification

    Inspections and sign-offs through to your Code Compliance Certificate.

  6. 6

    Handover or sale

    Keys in hand, ready to move in, rent or sell, backed by our guarantees.
