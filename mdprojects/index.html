Ask a question
What types of homes do you build?

Standalone houses, duplexes, terrace homes and full subdivisions, tailored to both homeowners and developers across the Auckland region.

How much will my build cost?

It depends on the design, location, materials and build complexity, so contact us for a detailed cost proposal. We offer fixed-price contracts with no hidden costs.

How long does a terrace build take?

Our typical build cycle for terrace houses is 7 to 9 months, depending on the complexity of the project and council approvals.

Do I need a resource consent?

A resource consent is needed when a project doesn't meet the permitted activity standards under Auckland Council's Unitary Plan. That typically includes subdividing into multiple lots, building height and boundary adjustments, land use changes, and stormwater and drainage connections.

What guarantees come with my home?

Every home we build comes with a 10-year Stamford Insurance Guarantee and a 12-month workmanship warranty.

How much deposit do I need for a development?

Development finance typically needs at least 25–40% equity, though this varies by lender and project size.

Can I bring my own plans?

Yes. With Build from Plans we bring your approved plans to life. If you don't have plans yet, our Design & Build service covers that too.
