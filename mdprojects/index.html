FAQs

Questions,
answered.

Can't see yours? Call 0210 590 409 or send us a message.

What types of homes do you build?+

Standalone houses, duplexes, terrace homes and full subdivisions, for homeowners and developers across the Auckland region.

How much will my build cost?+

It depends on the design, location, materials and build complexity, so contact us for a detailed cost proposal. Every contract is fixed-price with no hidden costs.

How long does a terrace build take?+

Our typical build cycle for terrace houses is 7 to 9 months, depending on the complexity of the project and council approvals.

Do I need a resource consent?+

You need one when a project doesn't meet the permitted activity standards in Auckland Council's Unitary Plan, for example subdividing into multiple lots, height and boundary changes, land use changes, and stormwater and drainage connections.

What guarantees come with my home?+

Every home comes with a 10-year Stamford Insurance Guarantee and a 12-month workmanship warranty.

How much deposit do I need for a development?+

Development finance typically needs at least 25–40% equity, though this varies by lender and project size.
