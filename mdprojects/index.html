FAQs

Questions we hear most.

Can't see yours? Give us a call or send a message and we'll talk it through.

Ask a question
What types of homes do you build?

Standalone houses, duplexes, terrace homes and full subdivisions, for homeowners and developers across the Auckland region.

How much will my build cost?

It depends on the design, location, materials and build complexity, so contact us for a detailed cost proposal. Every project is fixed-price, with no hidden costs.

How long does a terrace build take?

Our typical build cycle for terrace houses is 7 to 9 months, depending on the complexity of the project and council approvals.

Do I need a resource consent?

You need one when your project doesn't meet the permitted activity standards in Auckland Council's Unitary Plan. That usually covers subdividing into multiple lots, building height and boundary changes, land use changes, and stormwater and drainage connections. We'll confirm exactly what's needed during feasibility.

What guarantees come with my home?

Every home we build comes with a 10-year Stamford Insurance Guarantee and a 12-month workmanship warranty.

Can you subdivide my property?

Yes. We run the whole process: feasibility study, resource and building consents, engineering and infrastructure, construction and services, compliance and certification, then handover or sale.

How much deposit do I need for a development?

Development finance typically needs at least 25–40% equity, though this varies by lender and project size.

Can I bring my own plans?

Yes. With Build from Plans we price and build from your approved drawings. If you don't have plans yet, Design & Build covers that too.
