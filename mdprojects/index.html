FAQs

Good questions.

Building is a big decision. Here are the answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find yours? Just ask.

Ask us anything →
What types of homes do you build?

We build standalone houses, duplexes, terrace homes and full subdivisions, for both homeowners and developers, across the Auckland region.

How long does it take to build terrace homes?

Our typical build cycle for terrace homes is 7 to 9 months, depending on the complexity of the project.

Do I need a resource consent?

A resource consent is required when your project doesn't meet the permitted activity standards under Auckland Council's Unitary Plan. This typically applies to subdividing land into multiple lots, building height and boundary adjustments, land use changes, and stormwater and drainage connections. We'll tell you exactly what's needed during feasibility.

What guarantees come with my home?

Every home we build comes with a 10-year Stamford Insurance Guarantee and a 12-month workmanship warranty.

Can you help with subdividing my property?

Yes. We manage the full subdivision process: feasibility study, resource and building consents, engineering and infrastructure planning, construction and services installation, compliance and certification, and final handover or sale.

Can I bring my own plans?

Absolutely. Our Build from Plans service means we can price and construct from your existing approved drawings, or we can take care of design for you through Design & Build.
