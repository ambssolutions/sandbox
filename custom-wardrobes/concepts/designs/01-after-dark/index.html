What we make

Every piece is made for the room it goes in, with the lighting built in.

  • Marble-look panels, walnut and oak, slatted inserts, lit display towers and long floating units, with light around the TV and under the unit. One of our walls hides a prayer cabinet behind a door.

  • Carved arch frames, raised Gurbani lettering lit from behind, drawers below, lit glass shelves and floating benches. A single unit or a whole room.

  • Carved art and lettering panels with light set in behind, framed and fitted into a slatted or plain wall.

  • Walnut herringbone and slatted walls, padded headboards, storage beds lit underneath and bedside drawers to match.

  • Sliding, hinged or open walk-in, sized to your wall, with rails, shelves, drawers and lighting laid out around what you keep in it.

How it works

  1. 01

    Send a photo or give us a call

    Tell us which room it's for and roughly what you have in mind. A saved preview from the room studio, or a screenshot of one of our builds, is a good place to start.

  2. 02

    Measure and agree the design

    We check the space, then go through the layout, the finish, the carving and the lighting with you.

  3. 03

    Made and fitted

    Your piece is built to those measurements and installed in your home, lighting and all.
