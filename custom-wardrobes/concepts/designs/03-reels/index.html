Questions

Can I see any of your pieces in my own room?

Yes. In the room studio, take or choose a photo of your room, then pick one of our builds, cut a piece out of any photo in our gallery, or upload a screenshot of something you like. Drag its corners to fit. You can place up to four pieces at once.

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The room studio runs in your browser, so your photos stay on your phone or computer. They only reach us if you choose to send them.

How accurate is the preview?

It shows the style, colour and size in your space. It is not a measured drawing, and colours look different from screen to screen. Sizes, finishes and lighting are confirmed in person before anything is made.

How do I get a quote?

Call or text 020 4149 8811, or send the form below. A photo of the space, rough measurements and a saved preview all help.
