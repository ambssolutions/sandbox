How we look after your details.

Photos in the room preview

The room preview works entirely in your browser. When you take or choose a photo, it is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. It stays on your phone or computer, and it is gone when you close the page. If you save a preview image, it is saved to your own device.

Enquiries

When you send an enquiry, we collect the details you give us: your name, phone number, and any email, suburb, design or message you add. We use them only to reply to your enquiry and to quote and carry out the work you ask for. We don't sell or share them, except with a service that delivers the message to us.

Videos and social media

The videos of our own builds play from this website. The TikTok player on our page only loads if you choose to show it. TikTok's player may then set its own cookies, under TikTok's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the details we hold about you, and ask us to correct them. Call or text 020 4149 8811.
